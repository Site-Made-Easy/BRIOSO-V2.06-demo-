Four ways to sit

Pick a booth for a long talk, the garden for monsoon air, a window for the lane, or a lantern-lit table when the evening settles in.

🛋️

Indoor lounge

Deep crimson booth seating, soft pillows, round tables and lantern light. Built for long conversations, brunches, or focused laptop sessions.

🌳

Garden patio

Under old trees with red umbrellas and wooden chairs — monsoon evenings and sunny afternoons alike.

🪟

Window nooks

Seats by the large windows looking onto the lane — people-watching, cold brew in hand.

💡

Lantern tables

Smaller tables under paper pendants for a quieter evening pour, a pastry, and nowhere you need to be next.

The day

How the house shifts

Same rooms, different moods — from first bake to last layered pour.

Morning

7am – 11am

Croissants out of the oven, pour-overs on the bar, quieter booths for work. Best hour if you like the house to yourself.

Afternoon

11am – 5pm

Brunch plates, garden seating, matcha and coolers. The bar is lively; the patio is the move on a warm Pune day.

Evening

5pm – close

Lanterns on, music low, layered lattes and long tables. Come after work, stay through a second cup.

Atmosphere

Designed for feeling

A typical visit

What happens when you walk in

Greeted

A host finds you a booth, garden table, or window — no hovering, no rush.

Ordered

Bar and kitchen in one ticket. Ask what's roasting and what's just out of the oven.

Built

Drinks layered in view. Bakes plated warm. Nothing sitting under a heat lamp.

Settled

Stay for one cup or three. The rooms are built for lingering, not turning tables.

Back again

Regulars get remembered. First-timers get the same care. That's the house rule.
