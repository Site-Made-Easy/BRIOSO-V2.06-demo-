How it's made

From estate to your table

Origin

Beans sourced from small estates in Chikmagalur, Coorg and Baba Budangiri.

Roast

Small drum-roasted batches, tuned weekly to each lot's character.

Grind

Dialled in fresh per order on our on-bar grinders, never pre-ground.

Layer

Espresso, milk and flavour built in deliberate, visible layers.

Serve

Poured across the bar in Koregaon Park, usually within a minute of grinding.

Our story

More than coffee.
A place to belong.

Brioso started with a simple belief: Pune's specialty coffee deserved a space as considered as the cup itself. We roast with intention, brew with care, and build every drink in visible layers.

Whether you're here for a quiet morning pour-over or an evening under the Koregaon Park trees, every visit is meant to feel memorable.
