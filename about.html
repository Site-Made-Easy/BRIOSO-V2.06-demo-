The house

A bar built in layers

Brioso is a specialty coffee bar with an artisanal kitchen — small-batch Indian estates, laminated pastry, and drinks you can see being built. We roast with intention, brew with care, and host with warmth.

Whether you come for a quiet morning pour-over or an evening under the Koregaon Park trees, the visit is meant to feel memorable — not rushed, not generic, and never just a caffeine stop.

How we got here

From cart to house roast

2019

A cart at the weekend market

Brioso began as a single pour-over cart at a Koregaon Park weekend market — three flasks, one grinder, and a belief that Pune's coffee drinkers deserved better than instant.

2021

The first bar

We opened our first proper counter nearby, built around a two-group espresso machine and a menu of five drinks. Regulars started calling it "the layers place."

2023

Roasting in-house

We brought roasting on-site, sourcing direct from small estates in Chikmagalur and Coorg so every batch could be tuned to the bean, not the other way round.

2026

Roast & Layers

Today's space and menu — crimson booths, garden seating, a pastry counter, and a bar built around layered drinks — is the fullest version of that original cart yet.

What we stand on

Three words. Every cup.

01

Clean

Transparent sourcing and honest flavours — nothing hidden behind excess sweetness or smoke. You should taste the estate, not the marketing.

02

Premium

Specialty-grade beans, quality milk, ceremonial matcha, and equipment that respects the coffee in the cup. Small batches, dialled in daily.

03

Memorable

Warm lighting, thoughtful seating and hospitality that makes you want to come back — for the drink, and for how the hour felt.

The estates

Where the beans begin

We work with small lots from the Western Ghats — roasted in Koregaon Park, never as a generic blend dumped from a sack.

Chikmagalur

High-grown Arabica with citrus, cocoa and a clean finish. The backbone of our espresso and many of the layered lattes.

Coorg

Balanced lots with stone fruit and caramel — often on the pour-over bar when the week's roast is at its brightest.

Baba Budangiri

Smaller, rotating lots for guests who want something more floral or spice-forward. Ask the bar what's on this week.

The people

Who you'll meet

A small house team — bar, kitchen, and floor — so the same faces remember how you take your pour.

The bar

Roasters and baristas who grind to order, build layers in the glass, and talk origin without the lecture.

The kitchen

Bakers and cooks turning out sourdough, laminated pastry, and savoury plates from 7am until the last evening order.

The floor

Hosts who find you a booth, a garden table, or a window nook — and keep the visit unhurried even when the bar is busy.

What we believe

Coffee as connection

We're not just a café. We're a daily ritual for many — a place to work, meet friends, celebrate small wins, or simply sit with a good cup.
